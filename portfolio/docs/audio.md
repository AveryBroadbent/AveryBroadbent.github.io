## **[28 Leicester Square](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4493220/28_Leicester_Square/)**
(August 2025 - April 2026)

I handled and organized everything to do with sound in this project. That included:

- Production spreadsheet of every needed sfx (approved by our project lead)
- Designs of the sounds themselves
- Recording, Sampling, & foley work
- Audio balancing both out and out of engine
- Audio implementation (+ variation & pitch shifting)
- Custom audio system framework [(Engineering showcase)](engineering.md)
- Music composition and production [(Music showcase)](music.md)

This project was the first to truly let me flex my audio skills as my sole focus, instead of it being relegated to a skillset I had to leave on the backburner until the project "called for it" (which was often just moments before the final deadline). As such, the project stands as the single best reference point for my audio design skillset.

Out of everything in the project, around 60-70% of the original sound samples were either recorded or digitally generated by me. The rest were pulled from existing samples found on freesound.org. 

By the end of the project, I had made around **80+ finalized sound effects** for the final game.

### **-Ambience-**

The two ambience tracks are where the game gets most of its atmosphere. The first one here is the general ambience, an 8 minute loop that plays regardless of position. Interspersed are random bits of foley mostly from a general soundpack I had access to, along side some occasional drone samples I had lying around that are themselves unnerving to listen to.
